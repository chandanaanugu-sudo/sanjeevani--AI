07 • OP MEDIQUEUE (OUTPATIENT DEPARTMENT QUEUE)

OP MediQueue: Smart Outpatient Queue Pacing & Zero-Crowd Lobby

Full Form: OPD = Outpatient Department • MediQueue = Medical Queue Management & Pacing Engine

01

Dynamic Home-Waiting Pacing

Algorithms calculate travel time and consult pacing. Patients relax safely at home and arrive only when their turn is near ("Leave home at 10:30 AM for 10:50 AM consult").

02

Unified Token Sequence

Seamlessly merges online app appointments and offline walk-in kiosk tokens into a single fair, chronological queue (SANJ-104, 105, 106...).

03

Doctor Delay Broadcast (+15 Min)

When emergencies delay doctor by +15 mins, a single broadcast button notifies all patients at home, instantly shifting their departure times.
